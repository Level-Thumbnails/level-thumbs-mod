# Level Thumbnails

**Level Thumbnails** is a mod that adds thumbnails to levels in search.

## Thumbnail Submission

To submit thumbnails, go to **settings** and toggle "In-Game Thumbnail Taking."
Then take a thumbnail using the **Level Thumbnails** button in the **pause menu**.

### Checking your submission status:
1. Please go onto https://levelthumbs.prevter.me/ and log in with your **Discord** account.
2. Head to the settings *(on the website)* and copy your link token from the form displayed.
3. Open **Level Thumbnails** settings in the **Geode UI** and head to the option where it says "Link Account".
4. Paste the token you have just copied and press **Link**.
5. Go back on the website and refresh the page, you may now use the **Dashboard** and **My Thumbnails** tabs to see useful information about your prior submissions and **rejections**.
